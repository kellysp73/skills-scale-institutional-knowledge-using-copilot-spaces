# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A landing page for the project management process guidance in `docs/`.

## Project Management Processes Summary

OctoAcme manages cross-functional work through an iterative lifecycle: initiate and validate the business need, align stakeholders and success measures, plan scope and delivery, execute and track work, release and verify changes, then capture learnings through retrospectives. Initiation uses a project one-pager and a go/no-go decision; planning establishes a prioritized backlog, acceptance criteria, Definition of Done, milestones, dependencies, and risks.

Project Managers coordinate delivery schedules, risks, dependencies, and communications. Product Managers and Product Leads define outcomes, prioritize work, and measure success; developers build, test, document, and review increments; QA validates quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Teams use project boards and small, testable increments to keep work visible and delivery iterative.

Communication includes regular team standups, weekly PM and Product alignment or delivery syncs, monthly stakeholder updates, and demos at sprint or milestone ends. Teams maintain a shared status source and review risks regularly, escalating blockers from the team through the PM and Product Lead to the sponsor when needed. Quality practices include automated tests and linting in CI, security scanning, appropriate unit and integration tests, review and approval of pull requests, staging smoke tests, and post-deployment verification. Retrospectives turn lessons from sprints, releases, milestones, and incidents into owned, trackable improvements.
